**QUIZTON** is an AI-powered web app that helps educators of [St. Jude College Dasmariñas](https://sjcdc.phinma.edu.ph/) save time and effort by automatically transforming PDF lecture materials into customized quizzes. It solves the problem of manual quiz preparation through intelligent PDF conversion, a centralized question bank, and flexible export options — streamlining assessment and enhancing teaching efficiency.
