A mobile fitness application designed to gamify workouts and promote a healthy lifestyle. **ShapeShift** calculates a user's **BMI** and recommends personalized workout programs, allowing users to choose from challenge modes, track progress, and stay motivated through goal-driven features. Progress is synced with **Firebase**, and the app includes workout logs, timers, and visual trackers.

As **Lead Developer**, handled full-cycle development including Android UI design, logic implementation, and Firebase backend integration.
