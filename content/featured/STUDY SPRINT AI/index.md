**Study Sprint AI** is a smart Android flashcard app designed to help users **memorize faster** and **study smarter**. It leverages **AI-powered adaptive learning algorithms** and integrates tools like **OCR** and **ML Kit** for converting notes into flashcards. Users can track progress, study in Pomodoro cycles, and get performance feedback through clean analytics dashboards.

As **Lead Developer**, engineered the full app lifecycle, from OCR integration to adaptive flashcard generation and Firebase-based syncing.
